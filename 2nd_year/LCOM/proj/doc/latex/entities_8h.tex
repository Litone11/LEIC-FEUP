\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Entity management for player, bullets, enemies, and collisions. 

Declares structures and functions to handle player initialization and movement, bullet firing and updates, enemy spawning and movement, and collision detection.

Used in the main game loop to manage interactions and gameplay logic.
